## Image Specifications

### Specialist Photos
- **Format:** JPG (quality ~85)
- **Size:** 400-600px on the longer side; displayed at 60-120px in a circle
- **Aspect ratio:** 1:1 (square) preferred
- **Style:** Professional headshot, friendly expression

### Building Photos
- **Format:** JPG (quality ~82-85)
- **Size:** 1200px wide
- **Aspect ratio:** 3:2 or 4:3
- Keep files under ~300 KB; large originals should be resized before committing.
