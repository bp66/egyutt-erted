## Image Specifications (Recommended)

### Specialist Photos
- **Format:** JPG or WebP
- **Size:** 400x400px minimum (will be cropped to circle or square)
- **Aspect ratio:** 1:1 (square) preferred
- **Style:** Professional headshot, friendly expression

### Building Photos
- **Format:** JPG or WebP
- **Size:** 1200x800px minimum
- **Aspect ratio:** 3:2 or 16:9 for hero images

## Placeholder Image

For specialists without photos, use a generic silhouette or the center's logo as placeholder.
